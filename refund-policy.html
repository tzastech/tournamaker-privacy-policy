Refund Policy for Tournamaker

Last updated: November 2025

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for purchases made within the Tournamaker mobile application, operated by Tzastech S-Corp (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

By making a purchase in Tournamaker, you agree to this Refund Policy, in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to:

  • In-app purchases (such as participant tier unlocks, subscription features, and other digital tools); and
  • Tournament registration fees processed through third-party payment processors on behalf of tournament organizers.

All payments are processed securely by third-party providers such as Google Play Billing and payment processors (e.g., Stripe or similar providers). We do not store payment card numbers on our servers.

2. In-App Purchases (Digital Features)

Tournamaker offers in-app purchases for digital content and features, including (but not limited to):

  • Participant tier unlocks (tournament size limits)
  • Organizer tools or subscription-based features
  • Other digital enhancements within the app

These purchases are generally considered final and non-refundable once the product has been delivered or made available, except where required by the policies of the app store or applicable law.

A. Google Play Purchases

If you made a purchase through the Google Play Store, your transaction is governed by Google Play’s refund rules and terms. You may request a refund through Google Play here:

Google Play Refunds

We follow Google’s decisions regarding refunds for purchases made via Google Play. If Google grants a refund, your access to the related digital content or feature will be revoked.

B. Non-Refundable Digital Items

Unless required by app store policies or applicable law, the following are generally non-refundable:

  • Participant tier unlocks that have already been applied to your account
  • Subscription periods that have already begun
  • Digital tools or enhancements that have been delivered or used

3. Tournament Registration Fees

Tournamaker may allow tournament organizers to charge registration fees to participants. These payments are processed securely by third-party payment processors on behalf of organizers, with Tzastech S-Corp acting as the platform.

A. How Tournament Payments Are Handled

To protect participants from fraud and abuse:

  • Funds paid as tournament registration fees may be held by the payment processor on behalf of the platform and the organizer until the tournament is confirmed or completed.
  • Organizers do not receive immediate access to funds simply because a player has registered.
  • In the event of tournament cancellation or invalidation, refunds may be triggered according to this policy and the applicable payment processor’s rules.

B. Refunds for Cancelled or Deleted Tournaments

If a tournament for which you paid a registration fee is:

  • Cancelled by the organizer, or
  • Cancelled or removed by the platform (for example, for policy violations, fraud, or other issues),

we will generally initiate a refund of the registration fee to participants, subject to:

  • The policies of the payment processor or app store involved;
  • Applicable laws and regulations; and
  • Any irrecoverable processing fees charged by payment providers.

Our goal is to ensure that participants are not unfairly charged for tournaments that do not occur.

C. Refunds When Leaving or Being Removed

Refund eligibility for tournament registration fees depends on when the participant leaves or is removed and why the removal occurred. In general, refunds are more likely to be available before a tournament begins.

  • Participant leaves before the tournament starts:
    We may initiate a refund of the registration fee, subject to the payment processor’s rules and any non-recoverable processing fees.
  • Participant is removed (kicked) before the tournament starts:
    We may initiate a refund of the registration fee, subject to the payment processor’s rules and any non-recoverable processing fees, unless the participant was removed due to cheating, abuse, fraud, chargeback-related activity, or other violations of tournament or platform rules.
  • Participant leaves after the tournament starts:
    Registration fees are generally non-refundable once the tournament has started, except where required by law or where the organizer/platform determines a refund is appropriate under exceptional circumstances.
  • Participant is removed (kicked) after the tournament starts:
    Registration fees are generally non-refundable once the tournament has started, except where required by law or where the organizer/platform determines a refund is appropriate under exceptional circumstances.
  • Removal due to rule violations or misconduct:
    If a participant is removed due to cheating, harassment, abuse, fraud, or other violations of tournament rules or platform policies, registration fees are non-refundable.

Processing fees: Payment processors (such as Stripe) may charge processing fees (for example, a percentage of the payment and a fixed amount). These processing fees are typically non-refundable. When refunds are issued, the refunded amount may be reduced to account for any non-recoverable processing fees charged by payment providers.

D. No Cash Payouts for Unplayed Events

Participants are not entitled to additional compensation or damages beyond the refund of the registration fee (if applicable). Tournamaker and Tzastech S-Corp are not liable for travel costs, time, or other indirect expenses related to cancelled or modified tournaments.

4. Refund Requests and Disputes

A. How to Request a Refund

Depending on where you made your purchase:

  • Google Play: Request a refund directly through Google Play’s refund portal or support page.
  • Other payment processors (e.g., Stripe via web checkouts): Contact our support team at tzastech.corp@gmail.com with your payment receipt and account details.

We may ask for additional information to verify your identity and the transaction before taking action.

B. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you dispute a charge directly with your bank or payment provider (a “chargeback”), access to the related digital content, features, or tournament participation may be suspended or revoked. Repeated chargebacks or fraudulent claims may result in:

  • Restriction of certain features
  • Suspension of your account
  • Permanent removal from the platform in severe cases

5. Technical Issues and Failed Access

If you paid for a digital feature or tournament registration and:

  • Did not receive access, or
  • Experienced a technical issue preventing normal use,

please contact us promptly at tzastech.corp@gmail.com. In many cases, we will first attempt to restore access or fix the issue. If that is not possible, we may guide you through the appropriate refund process via the app store or payment provider.

6. Organizer Misconduct and Fraud Prevention

We take fraud and abuse seriously. If we determine that an organizer:

  • Repeatedly cancels tournaments after collecting fees,
  • Attempts to misuse funds, or
  • Engages in deceptive or abusive behavior,

we may:

  • Cancel or suspend their tournaments,
  • Restrict their access to payouts,
  • Issue refunds to affected participants where possible, and
  • Terminate their account and/or report activity to the relevant payment providers.

7. Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app, payment processors, or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of Tournamaker after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Refund Policy.
