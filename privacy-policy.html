Privacy Policy for Tournamaker

Last updated: January 2025

Tournamaker ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application operated by Tzastech S-Corp. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, compliant experience for all users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Tournamaker mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create a Tournamaker account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (securely hashed by Firebase; we never see it)
  • Username
  • Profile details (optional), such as profile picture or display name

B. Tournament & App Usage Data

When you create, manage, or join tournaments, we collect:

  • Tournament details (name, description, sport, format, rules)
  • Location and scheduling information you enter
  • Participants, teams, and admin roles
  • Messages, announcements, invites, and chat history related to a tournament
  • Your joined tournaments and participation status

C. Device & Technical Information

Collected automatically to help us improve performance and stability:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic and performance data

D. Payment & In-App Purchases

Tournamaker uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. We do not store payment card numbers. Google Play provides us with:

  • Transaction status (success, failure, refund)
  • Product purchased (e.g., participant tiers)
  • Purchase tokens for verification

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Tournamaker account
  • To enable tournament creation, participation, and administration
  • To facilitate chat, messages, invites, and notifications
  • To verify purchases and provide access to paid features
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share your information only in the following situations:

  • With tournament organizers, admins, and participants: when you join or interact with a tournament.
  • With Firebase services: for authentication, data storage, messaging, and analytics.
  • With Google Play Billing: to verify and fulfill in-app purchases.
  • If required by law: such as responding to valid legal processes.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firestore Database
  • Firebase Storage

These systems provide encryption in transit and at rest. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Choices & Controls

  • You may update or delete your account information at any time.
  • You may request account deletion via in-app settings or support.
  • You may adjust notification settings on your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

Tournamaker is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in the United States using Firebase servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
